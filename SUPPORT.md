# Support

Need help getting started, building, or using a Source Robotics project?  
Here’s how to get the right kind of help while keeping things efficient for everyone.

> 👉 **Note:** Before participating in our community, please read our [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/Source-Robotics/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).  
> By interacting with this repository, organization, or community, you agree to abide by its terms.

---

## How to Get Help

We love helping our community, but please follow these steps first:

1. :book: **Read the documentation**  
   - Read the manuals at [docs.source-robotics.com](https://docs.source-robotics.com/), including each manual's known issues page, and the README of the related project.
   - Explore any example projects to see how they work.  
   - Many questions are already answered in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/Source-Robotics/discussions), on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/prjUvjmGpZ) or in the older threads of our [forum](https://forum.source-robotics.com/), without waiting for a reply.
  
2. :mag: **Search before asking**  
   - Look for similar issues in the GitHub repository and in [Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/Source-Robotics/discussions).  
   - Search on [Stack Overflow](https://stackoverflow.com) for common problems (related to basic stuff like git, python, compilation errors...)
   - Use Google, ChatGPT or your favorite search engine for related terms.

3. :speech_balloon: **Ask in our community channels**  
   - [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/Source-Robotics/discussions/categories/q-a) – for questions, with answers that stay searchable for the next person  
   - [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/prjUvjmGpZ) – for quick chats and brainstorming  

4. :memo: **Found a bug? Open a GitHub Issue**  
   - Issues are for bugs in a design, the firmware or the software; questions go to Discussions.  
   - Clearly describe the problem.  
   - Include what you have already tried.  
   - Provide relevant code snippets or error messages (not your entire project).  

---

## Asking High-Quality Questions

Help us help you!  
A well-structured question saves everyone time.

- Clearly define your goal:  
  - *What are you trying to do?*  
  - *What’s the exact problem?*  
  - *What have you already tried?*
- Avoid the [XY Problem](https://meta.stackexchange.com/questions/66377/what-is-the-xy-problem/66378#66378) — ask about your actual goal, not just your attempted solution.
- Screenshots are helpful, but also include important text (like error logs) as plain text.

---

## What *Not* to Do

Please **do not**:
- ❌ Send technical questions by private message: ask in Discussions or on Discord instead, where public answers help everyone and more people can help you  
- ❌ Open duplicate issues or spam "+1" comments  
- ❌ Post vague questions without details or steps to reproduce  

Public questions get public answers — this helps the whole community.

## Help for customers

Bought a robot, a kit or a part from us? We help you get it running. We usually reply within three days.

- **Orders, shipping, returns and warranty:** email [info@source-robotics.com](mailto:info@source-robotics.com) or use the [contact page](https://source-robotics.com/pages/contact).
- **Building, wiring, setup and software:** ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/Source-Robotics/discussions/categories/q-a) or on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/prjUvjmGpZ). The team answers there, and the answer helps the next person too. Older questions and answers are on our [forum](https://forum.source-robotics.com/).
- **Custom integration or consulting:** contact us through the [website](https://source-robotics.com/pages/contact).
